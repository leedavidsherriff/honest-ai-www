Frequently Asked Questions

What's the typical cost of a project?
Projects typically range from £8,000 to £35,000 depending on scope, materials, and finishes. We offer flexible financing options with zero interest available for qualified customers. Your design consultation is free with no obligation.
Can you really complete it in 30 days?
Yes, our proven process is designed to deliver within 30 days from signed agreement. This includes design, ordering, and installation. We manage every detail so you don't have to worry about timelines.
What if I don't like the design?
We include unlimited revision rounds during the design phase. We don't move forward until you're 100% happy with the concept. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.
Do you work with smaller budgets?
Absolutely. We have packages starting at £5,000. During your free consultation, we'll discuss your budget and create a proposal that works for you.
What areas do you serve?
We currently serve the UK with installations in London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Birmingham, and surrounding areas. We're expanding rapidly — contact us to see if your area is covered.
